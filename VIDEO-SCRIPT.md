## Shots + Narration

1. **Title card** — "PowerHouse Gym — Owner App"
   VO: "This is PowerHouse Gym Owner App. One website that runs your whole gym — automatically."

2. **How it is made (simple words, over the login screen)**
   VO: "It is built on FastAPI, a fast and modern engine. Every member is saved safely in a database. And the full website is locked with a secure login — nobody can enter without a password."

3. **Signup page**
   VO: "A new gym owner first creates his account — name, gym name, email, and password."

4. **Details page**
   VO: "Next, he adds his gym details and his own owner details. Everything is saved — he never types it again."

5. **Upgrade page — plans**
   VO: "Then comes the upgrade page. Two plans — Starter at 499 rupees a month, and Pro at 999. He can switch between month and year. On the yearly plan, two months are free."

6. **Login → Dashboard**
   VO: "Now the owner logs in. This is his dashboard — total members, who came today, and the danger list — all automatic."

7. **SCREEN 1 — No-Show Red List**
   VO: "Screen one: the No-Show Red List. Thirty-two members have not come for more than ten days. Rohit has not come for sixteen days. The owner can call him in one tap — before the member leaves forever."

8. **SCREEN 2 — Customer QR check-in**
   VO: "Screen two: QR Check-In. The member shows his code… scans… and he is checked in. Watch — he disappears from the Red List automatically. Thirty-two becomes thirty-one."

9. **SCREEN 3 — Renewal**
   VO: "Screen three: Renewals. The app shows whose plan is finishing in seven days, and who already expired. One tap renews the member."

10. **SCREEN 4 — Add-Ons**
    VO: "Screen four: Add-Ons. Personal training, diet plan, sauna — the owner assigns, and the extra income adds up by itself."

11. **Search + Auto/Manual**
    VO: "Every section has a search bar — find any member in seconds. And if the owner wants full control, one switch changes the app from Automatic to Manual."

12. **End card** — "PowerHouse Gym — Owner App · Premium · Secure · Fully Verified"
    VO: "Premium look. Full security. Every section connected. This is PowerHouse Gym Owner App."
