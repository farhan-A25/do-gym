Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 October 2026 · Do Gym

This Privacy Policy explains what information Do Gym ("the App") collects, why it is collected, and the rights of gym members and the gym owner who uses the App. The App is a member-management tool used by a gym owner ("Owner") to manage memberships, attendance and add-on services.

1. Information we collect

  • Member details: member name, mobile phone number, membership plan (Monthly, Quarterly or Yearly) and membership expiry date.
  • Attendance records: the dates and times a member checks in at the gym.
  • Add-on services: extra services a member has taken, such as personal training or diet plans, and their monthly price.
  • Renewal records: dates and amounts of membership renewals.
  • Owner account: the Owner's email address and a securely hashed password (we never store the password in plain text).

We do not collect member payment card details, location data, or any information from members' own phones. The App uses no advertising or third-party tracking.

2. Why we collect it

  • To show the Owner which members have stopped attending, so the Owner can contact them.
  • To record daily check-ins and keep attendance history.
  • To remind the Owner about memberships that are due for renewal or have expired.
  • To calculate add-on service revenue.
  • To keep the Owner's account secure and signed in.

3. How information is stored and protected

Data is stored in a database on the server that runs the App. Passwords are stored only as strong bcrypt hashes. Access to member data requires the Owner to log in; there are no public pages that show member information. Login attempts are rate-limited to protect the account. We recommend the Owner uses a strong, unique password and logs out ("Exit") on shared devices.

4. Sharing of information

We do not sell, rent or share member personal information with any third party for marketing. Member phone numbers are used only so the Owner can call or message the member about their membership.

5. Cookies

The App uses exactly one cookie: a secure, signed session cookie that keeps the Owner logged in. It contains no personal member data, is never used for advertising, and is deleted when the Owner logs out.

6. Member rights

Any gym member may ask the gym Owner, at any time, to:

  • See what information about them is stored in the App;
  • Correct any wrong information (for example, a wrong phone number);
  • Delete their information from the App.

The Owner can fulfil these requests directly in the App, or by contacting us at the email below.

7. Data retention

Member records are kept while the person is a member of the gym and for a reasonable period afterwards for the gym's own records. The Owner may delete demo or old data at any time.

8. Children

The App is a business tool for gym owners. It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect information about children except where a parent or guardian has enrolled them as a gym member through the gym.

9. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.

10. Contact

For any privacy question or request, contact the gym owner at owner@powerhousegym.in.
